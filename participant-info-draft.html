Participant Information

Participant Information and Consent

Please read the following information carefully before deciding whether to participate in this research. You may ask the research team to clarify anything you do not understand.

1. About the Research

Study title: Development and Consumer Evaluation of an Eco-Cultural Brand Identity and Packaging Design for Laksoy

PROJECT L.A.K.A.S. is a research initiative that focuses on developing and evaluating proposed eco-cultural brand identity and packaging designs for Laksoy.

The study aims to gather feedback about the visual presentation, cultural representation, clarity, packaging quality, and overall design of the proposed concepts.

2. Who May Participate?

Eligibility requirement: Only individuals who are at least 21 years old may participate in this evaluation.

Individuals below 21 years old are not eligible to participate and must not proceed to the evaluation.

3. What Will Participants Do?

Participants will be invited to examine five proposed Laksoy packaging design presentations and provide feedback through the F1 evaluation questionnaire.

The evaluation may involve:

  • Viewing the proposed design presentations.
  • Assessing the visible design elements.
  • Answering the evaluation statements.
  • Providing comments about strengths and possible improvements.

The activity focuses on design impressions and observable design features.

Important: No alcohol tasting or consumption is involved. Participation does not require purchasing, selling, or buying any product.

4. Voluntary Participation and Your Rights

Participation in this research is entirely voluntary. You may:

  • Choose whether or not to participate.
  • Skip a question you do not wish to answer.
  • Select "Cannot Judge" when appropriate.
  • Stop participating without giving a reason.

Your decision will not affect your grades, services, or relationship with the researchers or school.

Because responses are collected anonymously and are not linked to participant identities, individual submitted responses may not be identifiable for removal after submission.

5. Privacy and Handling of Responses

This evaluation is designed to collect responses anonymously. Participants will not be asked to provide their names, signatures, learner reference numbers, or other direct identifying information through the evaluation questionnaire.

Responses will be used for research analysis. Findings may be presented through summarized results and de-identified comments.

Please avoid including your name or other personal identifying information in the written comment sections.

6. Research Details and Contacts

The following information describes the arrangements for this research:

  • School: Ilaya Barangka Integrated School
  • Evaluation date: November 20–25, 2026
  • Evaluation venue: School premises
  • Estimated total participation time: Approximately 15–20 minutes
  • Response collection date: November 25, 2026

Data Storage, Confidentiality, and Deletion: Each submitted evaluation will be assigned a unique submission ID to organize and distinguish response records. The evaluation questionnaire will not collect participants' names or learner reference numbers. Digital response records will be stored in password-protected storage, with access limited to the authorized research team and research adviser.

The collected responses will be used solely for this research. They will be retained only for the time necessary to complete data verification, analysis, and preparation of the final research report. After these activities are completed, the digital response records will be securely deleted, unless the school requires them to be retained for a specified period. Consent records will be kept separately from the evaluation responses by the designated adult coordinator.
