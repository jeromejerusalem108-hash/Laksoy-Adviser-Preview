Welcome to the Laksoy Design Evaluation

Thank you for taking the time to learn about our research.

PROJECT L.A.K.A.S. is a research initiative focused on the development and preliminary evaluation of Laksoy brand identity and packaging designs.

This evaluation aims to gather feedback on the visual presentation, cultural representation, clarity, and overall design of the proposed packaging concepts.

About This Evaluation

Through the evaluation process, eligible participants will be invited to view and assess five Laksoy packaging design presentations.

Your feedback will help identify the strengths of each design and areas that may need improvement.

Important Information

  • This website is intended for the F1 design evaluation.
  • Only individuals aged 21 and above may participate.
  • The evaluation concerns design impressions, not alcohol tasting or consumption.
  • Participation is voluntary.
  • No purchase, sale, or buying obligation is involved.

Before You Begin

Before proceeding, please read the Participant Information and Consent document carefully.

It explains the research, evaluation procedures, participant rights, and how responses will be handled.
