Participant Consent

Voluntary Participation and Anonymity

Your participation is voluntary. Your name and signature will not be requested through this evaluation website. Your responses will be handled anonymously.

1. Confirmation of Understanding

By reviewing this consent section, you acknowledge that you have been provided with information about the research, its procedures, and your rights as a potential participant.

You may ask the research team to clarify any part of the activity before deciding whether to participate.

2. Decision to Participate

After reading the Participant Information document, please indicate your decision.

3. Optional Permission for Quotations

This is a separate and optional decision. It concerns whether short, de-identified quotations from your comments may be included in research reporting. Your decision will not affect your participation in the study.
